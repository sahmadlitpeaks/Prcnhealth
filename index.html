§ VII · Frequently asked

Five things people always ask.

Anything else? Send us a note →

Is Precision Health Group itself a clinic?

No — the group is a parent brand. Our medical clinic is Precision Health Clinix. The group operates four brands together: Clinix, Wellness, Shop and Academy.

Where do I start if I just want a check-up?

Start at Talk to us. A care navigator will route you to a Clinix consult, an at-home lab kit or a Wellness centre tour.

Can my company partner with you?

Yes. See our Corporate Health Packages — built for teams from 50 to 5,000+.

Are the Academy courses certified?

Yes. Self-paced programmes carry CPD-aligned certificates; the residency is recognised as continuing professional development by partner accreditation bodies.

Do you accept my insurance?

Clinix accepts NAS, Neuron, Almadallah and FMC. New partners are added quarterly.
