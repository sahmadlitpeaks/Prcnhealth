Questions, answered

Before your first visit.

What is precision medicine — and how is it different?

Precision medicine uses your genetics, biomarkers and lifestyle data to design protocols built specifically for you — instead of population averages. You stop guessing, and start treating root causes.

Do you accept my insurance?

We currently accept NAS, Neuron, Almadallah and FMC, with new partners added quarterly. Selected diagnostics and consults are also offered on flexible self-pay packages.

Can I start without visiting a clinic?

Yes. Most journeys begin with a free 15-minute discovery call, followed by a telehealth consult and at-home lab kits. Hydrogen and IV therapies can also be delivered to your home.

How long until I feel a difference?

Most patients report meaningful changes in energy, sleep or pain within the first 3–6 weeks. Deeper biomarker shifts are typically reviewed at 90 days.

Are the supplements you sell the same ones I'd be prescribed?

Yes — PRCN Shop only carries the practitioner-grade brands and SKUs our clinicians actually prescribe in clinic. No filler products, no compromises.
