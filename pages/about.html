About · The Group

Built by clinicians
tired of guesswork.

Founded in Dubai in 2018, Precision Health Group is an integrated portfolio of four healthcare and wellness brands — each independent, all aligned around the same standard of evidence and care.

§ I · Mission

Why we exist

Achieving true wellness for humanity through integrative medicine, advanced lab and genetic insights, and personalised protocols designed for lifelong results.

Modern medicine treats the average patient. We exist for the italicised one — the person whose biology, lifestyle and goals don't fit a textbook.

That's why we built four brands instead of one — so wherever you start, the system already knows what to do next.
