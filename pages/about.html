§ I · Mission

Achieving true wellness for humanity through integrative medicine, advanced lab and genetic insights, and personalised protocols designed for lifelong results.

We started Precision Health Group in 2018 because the gap between what modern science can deliver and what most patients actually receive had become too wide to ignore. So we built four brands — Clinix, Wellness, Shop and Academy — that together can finally close it.
