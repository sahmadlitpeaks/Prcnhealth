Founded in Dubai in 2018, Precision Health Group is a portfolio of four healthcare and wellness brands — each independent, all aligned around one standard of care.

Mission

Achieving true wellness for humanity through integrative medicine, advanced lab and genetic insights, and personalised protocols designed for lifelong results.

Founder · Chair

Dr. Rania Khan

A note from the founder

We started Precision Health because the gap between what science can deliver and what most patients actually receive had become too wide to ignore. So we built four brands that, together, can finally close it.

Before founding the group, our team practised across the NHS, Cleveland Clinic and Dubai Healthcare City. We saw the difference between good care and complete care, and we built the second one.
