Our story

Built by clinicians who got tired of guesswork.

Precision Health Group is an integrated ecosystem — clinics, telehealth, advanced therapies and supplements — designed around a single belief: the only healthcare worth scaling is the kind that's truly personal.

Our mission is to help every patient move from suboptimal to truly well.

We pair the best of conventional and integrative medicine with proprietary diagnostics — genetic, microbiome and biochemical — to design protocols that target root causes, not symptoms. We call this approach the Diamond System of Care™.

It's powered by a team of doctors, nutritionists, coaches and researchers who refuse to settle for "your labs are normal" when patients clearly aren't.
